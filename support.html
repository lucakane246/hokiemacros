Common questions

Where is my data?

Only on your phone. There are no accounts. To move to a new phone, open You → Settings → Back up, save the file, then restore it on the new phone.

A menu or price is wrong.

Dining menus come from the university's dining system and refresh every morning, so they can change during the day. For prices, tap "Wrong?" on the item to send a correction.

How do I sync steps?

Tap the Steps card on Today and allow access in Apple Health (or Health Connect on Android). Gobble only reads your step count.

What is nightlife mode?

Tap the moon button on Today. You get downtown bars and late-night food on the map, a drink builder, and a day that runs until 3 AM so late-night food counts toward tonight. It's for users 21 and older, and it turns itself off the next day.

Why do drink calories look high or low?

They're based on standard 1.5 oz shots and regular mixer pours. Bars pour differently, so treat them as estimates.
