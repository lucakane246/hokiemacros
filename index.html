About Gobble

A free, ad-free nutrition tracker made by students for students on the Virginia Tech meal plan.

Your privacy

There are no accounts, ads or tracking. Everything you log stays on this phone. Only food searches (the search text) and price reports you choose to send leave your phone, and neither is tied to you. Privacy policy

Steps from Apple Health or Health Connect: if you turn on step syncing in the app, Gobble reads only your step count. It is shown in your log on this phone and never leaves it. Gobble doesn't write anything to Apple Health or Health Connect, and you can turn syncing off from the Steps card.

Where the data comes from

Dining menus, nutrition and hours: VT Dining Services, refreshed every morning.
Gym and pool hours: VT Recreational Sports.
Everyday and restaurant foods: U.S. Department of Agriculture, Agricultural Research Service, FoodData Central (fdc.nal.usda.gov).
Map: © OpenStreetMap contributors and Virginia aerial imagery.
Brand and restaurant foods: Powered by fatsecret.

Menus and hours can change without notice. Check signs in the dining center, especially for allergens.

Not medical advice

Calorie and nutrient numbers are estimates. Gobble isn't a substitute for advice from a doctor or registered dietitian.

Gobble is an independent student project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Virginia Tech.
