Privacy Policy

Gobble · Effective November 16, 2026
The short version: Gobble has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. Your food diary, goals, weight, water, drinks and steps are stored only on your phone. A few features need the internet (food search and price reports), and they send only what's needed to answer that request, never your name or your diary.

What stays on your phone

Everything you log: foods, meals, drinks, water, exercise, weight, steps, your goals and settings. This data is kept in the app's storage on your device. We can't see it, and it isn't backed up to any server. If you delete the app, it's gone unless you made a backup file using Settings → Back up.

Apple Health and Health Connect

If you turn on step syncing, Gobble reads your step count only from Apple Health (iPhone) or Health Connect (Android). Steps are shown in your log on your phone and are never sent off the device, shared, or used for advertising. Gobble does not write anything to Apple Health or Health Connect. You can turn syncing off in the app at any time, or in your phone's Health settings.

Your location

If you tap the location button on the map, Gobble uses your location to show where you are and how long it takes to walk to a dining hall, bar or restaurant. All of that is calculated on your phone. Your location is never sent anywhere or stored.

What is sent off your phone

  • Food searches. When you search for a brand or restaurant food, the search text (for example "big mac") goes to our small server (a Google Apps Script) and on to FatSecret, which provides the nutrition data. Results may be cached on the server for a few hours so repeat searches are fast. Searches aren't tied to you or your device.
  • Price reports. If you choose to report a price, Gobble sends the item, the price, the dining location and a random ID created by the app. The random ID is used only to stop spam and duplicate reports; it isn't connected to your name, your Apple ID, or your diary. Reports are reviewed by a student moderator and stored in a Google Sheet.
  • Menus, hours and how busy gyms are. The app downloads public dining menus, hours and gym occupancy. These downloads don't include anything about you.

Like any website or server, the services that answer these requests (GitHub Pages, Google and FatSecret) may see your device's IP address as part of normal network traffic. We don't collect or keep it.

What we don't do

  • No accounts, sign-ins or email collection.
  • No advertising and no ad or analytics trackers.
  • No selling or sharing of personal information.
  • No tracking across other apps or websites.

Nightlife mode

Nightlife mode is for users 21 and older. Drinks you log are stored on your phone like any other food. Drink and alcohol numbers are estimates based on standard pours.

Children

Gobble is meant for college students and isn't directed to children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update it here and change the date at the top.
